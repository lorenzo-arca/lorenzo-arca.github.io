abstract: |
  I build a structural model in which heterogeneous firms choose between four types — domestic, exporter, sourcer, and global — under Cobb-Douglas production with a CES composite of differentiated labor inputs. The framework, calibrated to French matched employer–employee data (DADS-Postes), customs flows (Douanes), and firm balance sheets (FICUS/FARE), identifies how monopsony power in local labor markets shapes the offshoring decision and its distributional consequences.
